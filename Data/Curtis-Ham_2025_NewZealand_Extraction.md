## Independent_Variables

**Value**: Activity location variables (25 total): reliability-relevance categories (4) × distance bands (6) + opportunity variable (1)

Activity location categories (based on reliability and relevance):
1. High reliability/High relevance nodes within SA2
2. High reliability/Low relevance nodes within SA2
3. Low reliability/High relevance nodes within SA2
4. Low reliability/Low relevance nodes within SA2
5-24. Same 4 categories across 5 distance bands (0-200m, 200-500m, 500m-1km, 1-2km, 2-5km)
25. Opportunity variable (crime-type specific)

**Reliability index components** (averaged):
- Frequency of visits
- Recency of visits
- Duration of residence/activity

**Relevance index components** (averaged):
- Behavior similarity
- Location type similarity
- Hour of day similarity
- Day of week similarity
- Season similarity

**Supporting Quotes**:
- "For each crime type we ran a conditional logit model involving four reliability-relevance categories (HfHs, HfLs, LfHs, LfLs)×6 distance bands...+1 opportunity variable = 25 estimates" (p. 17)
- "we averaged the reliability variables (frequency, recency, and duration) and relevance variables (behavior, location type, hour, day, and season similarity)" (p. 16)

**Reasoning**: 25 independent variables per model: 24 activity location variables (4 reliability-relevance categories × 6 distance bands) + 1 opportunity control variable.
